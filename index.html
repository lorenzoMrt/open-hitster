🎄 Open Hitster

How to play

  1. Everyone starts with one card that shows its year face-up.
  2. On your turn a mystery song plays. Place it in your timeline: is it older, newer, or somewhere in between your cards?
  3. Lock it in. Anyone else with a token can shout HITSTER! and pay 1 🪙 to put the song somewhere else in your timeline.
  4. Reveal. If you were right, you keep the card. If you were wrong but a challenger got it right, they get the card for their own timeline.
  5. Name the artist and title before the reveal to earn 1 🪙 (max 5).
  6. Spend 1 🪙 to skip a song you don't like, or 3 🪙 to buy a card that goes straight into your timeline.
  7. The first player to collect the target number of cards wins!

Two songs from the same year can go in either order.
